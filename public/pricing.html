Frequently asked questions

Can I try before I pay?
Yes — the Free plan lets you write up to 5 diary entries with no credit card required. Upgrade whenever you need more.
What happens to my entries if I downgrade?
All your existing entries are preserved. You'll just be limited to creating new entries once you reach 5. No data is ever deleted.
What file types can I upload?
Pro and Lifetime plans support JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP images and MP4, WebM, OGG, QuickTime videos up to 200 MB per file.
Is the Lifetime plan really one-time?
Yes. Pay $99 once and you get unlimited access forever — including all future features at no extra cost.
Can an admin give me a free upgrade?
Yes — admins can assign any paid plan to a user at no charge, with or without an expiry date. This is used for early supporters, contributors, or special promotions.
